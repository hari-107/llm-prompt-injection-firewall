Version 1.0 | Effective October 8, 2026

This is a fictional sample policy for testing the project's Rules uploader. It is
not the policy of a real AI provider and is not legal advice.

1. Using the service
You may use the service to draft, summarize, translate, analyze, and learn from
content that you have the right to submit. You are responsible for reviewing
outputs before relying on or sharing them. AI outputs may be inaccurate.

2. Account and content responsibilities
You must keep your account credentials secure. You must have permission to
submit any personal, confidential, or copyrighted material. Do not submit
sensitive information unless you are authorized to do so.

3. Prohibited uses
You may not use this service to generate phishing emails or deceptive login
messages designed to steal credentials.

You may not use this service to create malware, ransomware, or malicious code
intended for unauthorized access or distribution.

You may not use this service to write fraudulent impersonation messages that
pretend to be a real person or organization to obtain money or information.

You may not use this service to produce spam messages for unsolicited bulk
delivery.

You may not use this service to produce instructions for making explosives or
weapons for violent wrongdoing.

You may not use this service to create sexual content involving minors.

You may not use this service to generate instructions for self-harm or suicide.
If someone may be in immediate danger, seek local emergency or crisis support.

You may not use this service to create targeted harassment or threats against
an identifiable person.

4. Enforcement
The provider may block requests that violate this policy, limit access, or
suspend accounts after reviewing suspected abuse. Users may appeal a decision
through the provider's support process. A firewall implementing this sample
policy should block matching prohibited requests while leaving unrelated
requests available.

5. Data and privacy
The provider processes submitted content to provide the service and maintain
safety. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal data and should
follow applicable privacy obligations. This sample makes no promise about
retention periods or training use by any real provider.

6. Changes
The provider may update these terms and show the effective date of the latest
version. Continued use after an announced update indicates acceptance where
applicable law permits.
